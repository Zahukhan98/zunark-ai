## Internal linking rules

- Every article links to at least one Service and, where relevant, one Solution.
- Every article ends with the "Discuss Your Project" CTA.
- Comparison articles (#1, #4) cross-link to each other.
- Cost articles (#7, #9, #11, #13) link to the relevant Service/Solution pair, never to each other, to avoid cannibalizing the same commercial intent.
- Industry-specific articles (#12, #13) link to `/industries/healthcare`.

## Cannibalization check

No two planned articles target the same primary keyword. Cost-related articles are split strictly by product (custom software / AI / CRM / healthcare system) so they don't compete against each other in search.
